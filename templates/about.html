About AI Resume Enhancer & ATS Analyzer

Full-stack Python & AI web application engineered for intelligent resume optimization and ATS parsing compliance.

System Architecture & Philosophy

Bridging the Gap Between Job Seekers and Automated Hiring Algorithms

Modern Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) like Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, and Lever filter out up to 75% of qualified resumes before human eyes ever see them. Most rejections are not due to lack of ability, but due to unindexed keywords, missing section markers, passive sentence structures, and complex document formatting.

AI Resume Enhancer & ATS Analyzer was developed to give candidates institutional-grade tools to audit their documents, align with specific job descriptions, and apply modern AI rewriting without ever inventing false experiences or compromising truthful integrity.

Technology Stack & Module Responsibilities

Flask Backend Engine

Modular Python backend structured across specialized services (`resume_parser.py`, `ats_analyzer.py`, `job_matcher.py`, `ai_enhancer.py`, `pdf_generator.py`).

PyMuPDF & python-docx

Native C-speed PDF parsing and Word XML text extraction that accurately extracts text, paragraph breaks, tables, and headers without external dependencies.

Google Gemini API

Integration via modern `google.genai` SDK using STAR prompt constraints to rewrite summaries, experience descriptions, and project achievements with zero hallucinations.

ReportLab PDF Studio

Programmatic PDF generation generating three distinct professional templates: Modern Blue, Executive Slate, and Minimalist Tech.

SQLite Persistence

Zero-configuration relational database storing resumes, structured JSON payloads, ATS scoring audits, and AI enhancement history with foreign key integrity.

Bootstrap 5 & Vanilla JS

Clean responsive dashboard UI with deep navy and royal blue palette, custom circular progress gauges, live preview synchronization, and drag-and-drop file upload.

ATS Scoring Criteria & Weights

Category Weight Evaluation Methodology
Section Completeness 20% Presence of standard ATS markers: Contact info, Professional Summary, Work Experience, Technical Skills, and Education.
Skills & Keyword Variety 25% Detection of industry-recognized skills across Languages, Frameworks, Cloud/DevOps, Databases, and Tools with domain breadth analysis.
Experience Quality & Metrics 25% Density of strong past-tense action verbs (e.g. Spearheaded, Engineered), presence of quantifiable metrics (%, $, scale), and absence of passive phrases.
Formatting & Readability 20% Word count calibration (350 - 950 words ideal), structured bullet points instead of prose walls, and standard typography boundaries.
Contact & Online Presence 10% Verified presence of email, telephone number, LinkedIn profile URL, and GitHub / developer portfolio links.
